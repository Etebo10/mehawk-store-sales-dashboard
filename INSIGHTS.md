# Insights and Recommendations

All figures come from the full dataset (2011-2014, 9,994 order lines) and match the live formulas in the workbook.

## 1. Discounting is destroying profit

| Discount band | Order lines | Sales | Profit | Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| None | 4,798 | $1,087,908 | $320,988 | 29.5% |
| Up to 10% | 94 | $54,369 | $9,029 | 16.6% |
| 10-20% | 3,709 | $792,153 | $91,756 | 11.6% |
| 20-30% | 227 | $103,227 | -$10,369 | -10.0% |
| 30-40% | 233 | $130,911 | -$25,448 | -19.4% |
| 40-50% | 77 | $64,404 | -$22,999 | -35.7% |
| Over 50% | 856 | $64,229 | -$76,559 | -119.2% |

- 18.7% of order lines lose money (1,871 lines, $156,131 in total losses).
- 72% of those loss-making lines carry a discount above 20%, and they account for 89% of the money lost.
- **Recommendation:** cap discounts at 20% unless approved. This is the single biggest profit opportunity in the data.

## 2. Product performance

- **Top by sales:** Phones ($330,007) and Chairs ($328,449). Nine of the 17 sub-categories generate 80% of all sales.
- **Top by profit:** Copiers ($55,618, 37.2% margin). Labels, Paper and Envelopes have margins above 40%.
- **Loss-makers:** Tables (-$17,725), Bookcases (-$3,473) and Supplies (-$1,189) lose money overall despite healthy sales.
- **Best single product:** Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier ($61,600 in sales, $25,200 profit).
- **Worst single product:** Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head Print (-$8,880).
- **Recommendation:** review pricing and supplier costs for Tables, Bookcases and Supplies, and push high-margin lines harder than their sales rank suggests.

## 4. Growth and seasonality

| Year | Sales | Profit | Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2011 | $484,247 | $49,544 | 10.2% |
| 2012 | $470,533 | $61,619 | 13.1% |
| 2013 | $608,474 | $81,727 | 13.4% |
| 2014 | $733,947 | $93,508 | 12.7% |

- Sales grew 52% from 2011 to 2014, with a small dip in 2012. Profit grew faster than sales.
- November ($349,120), December ($332,177) and September ($309,770) are the strongest months. February is the weakest ($60,173).
- October to December delivers 38% of annual sales.
- **Recommendation:** plan stock, staffing and promotions around Q4. Use January and February for clearance and planning.
